Normals Under Non-Uniform Scaling

Lighting needs a normal at each vertex: a unit vector perpendicular to the surface. Both spheres below are scaled by the same model matrix M = scale(sx, sy, sz); they differ only in how the vertex shader transforms their normals. Move the scale sliders and compare the two views, then follow the steps.

Scale (model matrix M)

One set of sliders scales both spheres.

View

These move the camera around both spheres. They don't change the model matrix.

Show

The light comes from the upper left, in front. With shading off, the spheres are flat gray.
