Click anywhere on the cube. The cube stops, and this panel follows the fragment under the mouse through the code: which triangle it is in, its weights, the three colors, the rule that colors it, the lighting, and the pixel that was really drawn.

Good to know

The time slider is uTime. It also turns the cube, as in tabs 5 and 6 (angle = time × 0.3). It goes back to 0 after 72 s.

While the cube is stopped you can change the menus or drag the time slider. The trace follows the same pixel.

The trace is worked out in JavaScript (a copy of the shader’s math) and then compared with the pixel WebGL really drew. On the border between two branches, rounding can send the GPU the other way, so the two may differ.
