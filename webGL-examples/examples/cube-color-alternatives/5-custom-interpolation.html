5. Do-it-yourself interpolation

Same colors as tab 4, but this time the fragment shader does the interpolation. Each vertex carries its barycentric coordinates, (1,0,0), (0,1,0) or (0,0,1), plus all three vertex colors of its triangle. So every fragment (candidate pixel) receives its own weights λ0, λ1, λ2 and the three colors, and mixes them however it likes.

Try "Nearest vertex" or "Wireframe" to see the two triangles on each face. Then write your own rule in interpolate().
