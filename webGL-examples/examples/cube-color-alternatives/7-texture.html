7. A texture

Like tab 3, but each vertex carries a texture coordinate (u, v) instead of a color: the point of the brick image that belongs at that vertex. The rasterizer interpolates (u, v) across each triangle, just as it interpolated colors, and the fragment shader looks up each fragment's color in the image with texture().

Every face gets the image's four corners at its four cube corners, so each face shows the whole picture once.
